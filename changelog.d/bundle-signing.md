**Sign a macOS app bundle, inside-out, for the internal audience** (ZEOCORE-SOW-32). `release.yaml`'s `signing` section, at `release_manifest: 2`, adds `bundles`: a `.app` that keeps its own `CFBundleIdentifier` as its signing identifier, with every nested Mach-O and nested bundle listed by path, kind and digest, codeless resource bundles included. A bundle's main executable, at every level, is declared and hashed, signed and verified with its bundle under its identifier, and never listed. Before signing, the step walks the bundle and refuses unlisted or missing code, a changed tree or Info.plist identifier, and any link that leaves the bundle. It signs a copy deepest first, then the bundle, never with `--deep`, and writes a receipt per item plus a bundle receipt. `verify` and `receive` check every nested item and then the whole bundle with `codesign --verify --strict --deep`, and record `spctl --assess` as information only. Packs carry the whole bundle tree. Links are allowed only as a framework's `Versions/Current` and top-level links, each hop resolved inside the framework, and codesign is never pointed through one. Signed entitlements must now equal the manifest's exactly: `none` means no entitlements blob, and an empty entitlements file is refused, for executables too. A grant names an outer bundle's identifier apart from its `ac.zeo` ones. `zeocore release tree-digest` prints a bundle's canonical tree digest. Version 1 manifests are unchanged, and no product is granted bundle signing yet: zeoapp's allowlist entry awaits review.
