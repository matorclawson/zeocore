**Sign a macOS app bundle, inside-out, for the internal audience** (ZEOCORE-SOW-32). `release.yaml`'s `signing` section, at `release_manifest: 2`, adds `bundles`: a `.app` that keeps its own `CFBundleIdentifier` as its signing identifier, with every nested Mach-O and nested bundle listed by path, kind and digest, codeless resource bundles included. A bundle's main executable, at every level, is declared and hashed, signed and verified with its bundle under its identifier, and never listed. Before signing, the step walks the bundle and refuses unlisted or missing code, a changed tree or Info.plist identifier, and any link that leaves the bundle. It signs a copy deepest first, then the bundle, never with `--deep`, and writes a receipt per item plus a bundle receipt. `verify` and `receive` check every nested item and then the whole bundle with `codesign --verify --strict --deep`, and record `spctl --assess` as information only. Packs carry the whole bundle tree, with relative links contained in it. `zeocore release tree-digest` prints a bundle's canonical tree digest. Version 1 manifests are unchanged, and no product is granted bundle signing yet: zeoapp's allowlist entry awaits review.
