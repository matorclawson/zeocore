**HubSpot drafts can take replies at another address.** `EmailDraft` gains an optional `reply_to`, sent as HubSpot's `from.customReplyTo`, so a newsletter can go out from a connected sending domain while replies go elsewhere. HubSpot's `from.replyTo` stays both the from address and, without `customReplyTo`, the reply-to. A draft without `reply_to` sends exactly the payload it sent before. The reply-to is part of the reviewed send specification, so changing it after approval refuses the send. Requested by zeonewsroom; checked against HubSpot's published Marketing Emails spec (2026-09 rollout).
