**Read review comments on a Google Drive file.** `GoogleDriveService.list_comments(file_id)` and `list_replies(file_id, comment_id)` wrap Drive v3's `comments.list` and `replies.list`, read-only. They return typed `DriveComment` and `DriveReply` values: author, content, the quoted text a comment anchors to, its anchor, `resolved`, a reply's `resolve` or `reopen` action, times and `deleted`. Every page is followed to the end, and a listing that is too long, loops or holds a malformed item is refused rather than returned in part. Comment text is untrusted data. Drive's `comments.list` needs `drive.readonly`, `drive.file` (for files the app created or was granted), `drive.meet.readonly` or `drive`.
